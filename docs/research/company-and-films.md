## The company

**4est Films LLC** — https://www.4estfilms.com/ (Instagram: [@4estfilms](https://instagram.com/4estfilms))

Founded by **Joseph Bezenek** after working on projects in LA, London,
Minnesota, and Arizona. Per his IMDb bio, 4est Films exists to collaborate
with "wonderfully talented artists they have met along their journey in
order to produce their own, new art" — the stated filter being to only do
work he is "overwhelmingly passionate about."

Base of operations reads as Minneapolis/Twin Cities (Jeromy Darling is
Minneapolis-based; *Strung* won Best in Fest at Minneapolis St. Paul
International Film Festival).

### Story & origin

The story was **conceived by Jeromy Darling**, drawn from his own life:
he has worked in rehab centers and used music to heal since he was
sixteen. Over thirty collaborators brought it to life. It is Bezenek and
Reid's directorial debut.

The film's public statement closes with a suicide/addiction resource:

> If you or anyone you know is struggling with addiction, please call the
> SAMHSA National Helpline at **1-800-662-4357**.

**This helpline belongs on the site's *Strung* page.** It is on both the
official site and the Munich Film Awards listing — it's part of how the
film presents itself, not an afterthought.

### Themes for the design

Compassion, forgiveness, addiction and recovery, healing the inner child.
From the statement: *"Never give up on that child within, so we may
understand, teach, and raise our own children with the love they deserve."*

## Film 2 — *His Name is Michael* (in production)

- Instagram: [@hnimfilm](https://instagram.com/hnimfilm)
- Old site: `hisnameismichael.live` — **domain no longer resolves** (dead;
  content below is from a 2022 cached copy). The new site should absorb this.

### Logline

> The only priest in Nevada battles demons and outlaws for the soul of a
> boy with no name — an original scifi/western-themed musical by Jeromy
> Darling.

### Detail

Set in **Virginia City, Nevada, circa 1867**, shortly after the mining
bonanza that made it the most important industrial city between Denver and
San Francisco. Loosely based on **Father Patrick Manogue**, the only priest
in the state, who earned a reputation as *"Wyatt Earp with a collar"* —
feared by disreputable men, loved by everyone he helped.

The film "seeks to answer a question: **what is your name?**"

- **Jeromy Darling** — playwright, and stars as Father Manogue. This is his
  first foray into playwriting.
- **Wyatt Darling** — 6-time award-winning child actor, co-stars.
- Original music by **Jeromy Darling and Kurt Larson**.
- Part of a **trilogy** of scifi-themed stories each set 100 years apart.

Genre stack — scifi + western + musical + faith — is unusual and is the
most distinctive design hook on the whole site.

## Design implications

1. **Two films, very different tones.** *Strung* is intimate, grounded,
   recovery-focused. *His Name is Michael* is a scifi/western/musical.
   One visual system has to hold both — likely a neutral company shell
   with a strong per-film identity inside it.
2. **Lead with the laurels.** 12 wins is the strongest single credibility
   signal. A laurel wreath row is the obvious hero element.
3. **The artists are the throughline** — the same small group writes,
   directs, scores, and stars. Bio depth is a feature, not filler.
4. **Faith is not incidental.** Frassati, the retreats, a priest
   protagonist, "what is your name?" — the work is openly Catholic. The
   design should not sand that off.
5. **Absorb the dead HNIM site.** `hisnameismichael.live` is gone; that
   content has no home right now.
6. **Match jeromydarling.com.** Already animated and well-built — the new
   site should feel like a sibling.

### *Strung* — the real synopsis and credits

> Lost in the forest of his own mind, **Eric** must rely on the survival
> skills of an enigmatic nine-year-old boy to help him battle vicious
> withdrawals and self-deprivation to catalyze change within his family's
> cycle of addiction.

Use this as the logline — it is the film's own words and much stronger
than the directors' statement for a hero slot. The protagonist is named
**Eric**, played by Jeromy Darling; the nine-year-old is **Wyatt Darling**,
his real son, in his first-ever film role.

Crew surfaced from captions: DP **Alexander C. Shields**; AD **Molly
Worre**; steadicam **Travis Higgins** (@tmhigginsdp); stills **Forrest
Wasko**; additional photography **Julian McPherson**.

### *Strung* — awards, with the human detail

- **Jeromy Darling won the ONLY acting award at the Munich Film Awards**,
  out of **4,000+ submissions**. That is the single best line on the site.
- Jeromy also took "Best Performance of Fest" and "Best Actor in a Drama"
  at Actors Awards LA.
- **Wyatt Darling (age 9) won Best Child Actor *and* Best Supporting
  Actor** at Actors Awards LA — the latter beating adult performances in
  feature films. Plus Best Young Actor at LA Film Awards, Gold Award for
  Best Child Actor at Independent Shorts, and Best Young Actor of the Year
  at Top Shorts. Four acting awards in one month, in his first film.
- **Best First Time Director** at Top Shorts for Bezenek + Reid.
- Best of Fest at MSPIFF in its 40th year.

### *Strung* — the premiere, and why it matters

July 2021, the historic 1920s **Sioux Falls State Theatre**. Two nights,
red carpet, Q&A with stars, directors and producers. Tickets were free
with a donation — and the premiere **raised nearly $15,000** for
[Emily's Hope](https://emilyshope.charity/) and **Tallgrass Recovery &
Sober Living Homes**, to help people who cannot afford addiction
treatment. Sponsors: Avera McKennan Hospital, First Premier Bank &
Premier Bank Card.

This is the story the site should tell about *Strung* — not just that it
won things, but that the team turned it into money for treatment. Pair it
with the SAMHSA helpline.

A **feature-length version of *Strung* is in development** (per Emily's
Hope, 2021).

### *His Name is Michael* — production record

Shot over **9 days in November 2023** (wrapped Nov 24, 2023), directed by
Joseph Bezenek (@bezzbunny), DP **Aaron Berger** (@aaronjberger). 4est
Films' feature-film production debut. **The story began life on stage the
prior year and was so well received it had to be turned into a movie.**

The @hnimfilm feed is a day-by-day production diary — 131 images of a
9-day shoot. This is the richest visual material available and would carry
a "making of" section on its own. Highlights: Day 1 shot only 40% of
schedule but captured the film's opening; Day 3 fought wind, nearby
drilling and an out-of-tune piano; Day 4 "the most difficult to date — and
the most rewarding"; Day 6 went from children in an 1890 classroom to a
demonic presence in a barn, and the director cried on set for the first
time; Day 7 brought 20 child actors; Day 9 was a skeleton crew shooting
guerrilla-style by the Missouri River with a horse named "Hollywood."

Bezenek also appears on screen as **The King of Virginia City**. Other
characters named: Sister Mary Catherine, Gregory. Young actor **Zelalem**
wrapped on Day 5.

The tone throughout is explicitly prayerful — "by the grace of God,"
"send a prayer for us." Consistent with the faith thread noted above.
